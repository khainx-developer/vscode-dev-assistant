# Daily Tasks

The dashboard opens on your **Attention Inbox**: everything that needs you, highest priority first.

- **Red** — failing checks, changes requested, merge conflicts, overdue or stale work.
- **Blue** — new comments you haven't read.
- **Orange** — reviews you owe, tickets in review, due today or tomorrow.

Snooze anything you can't act on yet. The status bar shows how many items need you, and DevMate notifies you when something new arrives.
